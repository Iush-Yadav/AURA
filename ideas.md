# AURA — Cinematic Direction

## Three possible approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Black Mirror, Warm Signal** | A dark editorial film where warm human portraiture interrupts stark near-black information spaces. It makes technology feel intimate rather than cold. | 0.07 |
| **Acid Observatory** | A high-contrast research-art direction built around tracer lines, fluorescent annotations, and the language of observation. It frames self-knowledge as a living investigation. | 0.03 |
| **Luminous Index** | A pale, archival system with soft paper texture, clinical marks, and close-cropped evidence. It would make AURA feel precise, private, and quietly luxurious. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach — Black Mirror, Warm Signal

### Design Movement

**Experimental editorial cinema** informed by title-sequence pacing, print-layout tension, and humane future-health art direction. The site must unfold like a short digital film rather than a product brochure.

### Core Principles

1. **Mystery before explanation.** The mask, quiet, visual interruption, and large type establish emotional curiosity before AURA is named.
2. **Human warmth in a dark system.** The supplied character portrait provides the sole human anchor; warm skin tones cut through the near-black canvas.
3. **Typography carries the plot.** Massive grotesk phrases act as scenes, while serif notes behave like restrained internal narration.
4. **Information becomes calm.** Chaotic sources and questions collapse into clear, measured structures that express AURA’s central promise: understanding over advice.

### Color Philosophy

Near-black `#080808` creates the silence of an unopened cinema. Warm off-white `#F3F0E8` is used as a human, editorial contrast—not generic white. Acid green `#C8FF00` is an intentional interrupt, reserved for signals, active states, and the moments when AURA turns confusion into clarity. Peach and amber hues sampled in spirit from the supplied portrait add humanity without becoming a conventional beauty palette.

### Layout Paradigm

Use a **cinematic vertical montage**: full-bleed scenes, type-led pauses, horizontal rails, and off-axis captions. The site avoids a centered landing-page stack; sections alternate between immersive media, oversized text fields, asymmetric detail columns, and scrolling evidence lines.

### Signature Elements

1. **The signal line:** a luminous acid-green horizontal rule that appears as a reading position, timeline, or active stage.
2. **Editorial timecodes:** small monospaced labels such as `01 / THE MASK`, giving every scene the logic of a film edit.
3. **Soft portrait apertures:** the real-identity image emerges through elliptical crops, hard shadow edges, and then becomes fully present.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should make discovery feel deliberate. Hovering exposes annotations; scroll positions assemble and settle text; the waitlist action opens a quiet overlay rather than diverting visitors away. Motion always clarifies the transition from noise to understanding.

### Animation

The hero video fades in slowly through a high-contrast black veil. During the reveal, its scale softens while the supplied portrait rises into the frame and sharpens. Word clusters drift only in the "questions" and "noise" scenes, then visibly resolve. Progress markers animate in staggered 40–70 ms intervals with a strong ease-out. All decorative animation is disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the primary voice: compact, modern, and confrontational at display scale. **DM Mono** handles timecodes, system labels, and technical observation. **Cormorant Garamond** appears only for isolated reflective phrases, in italic, as an editorial counterweight. Headlines use tight tracking and deliberate breaks; paragraphs remain wide-set and readable.

### Brand Essence

**AURA is the coming personal intelligence layer for people who want to understand their changing skin, hair, and progress—not be sold another answer.**

**Personality:** observant, humane, unafraid.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are sparse, declarative, and slightly provocative. CTAs are invitations, never growth-hack commands. Microcopy acknowledges complexity without making a visitor feel defective.

> “Too much advice. Not enough understanding.”

> “The real you was never the problem.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is an intentionally tightened all-caps **AURA**, with the crossbar removed from the first A in concept to suggest an opening aperture. The standalone mark is a sharp, fragmented `A` aperture: a simple acid-green signal glyph that can live in the nav and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color

**AURA Signal — `#C8FF00`**

## Style Decisions

- **Human imagery:** The supplied real-identity portrait is kept as AURA’s only person. It is treated as a close, warm, intimate film still rather than as a generic beauty render, and no stock or generated people are introduced.
- **Signal color:** `#C8FF00` is reserved for activation, evidence, decisive emphasis, and the core invitation. It does not serve as a general-purpose section background.
- **Pacing:** Loud typographic scenes are separated by quiet observational pauses shaped with negative space, a timecode, and restrained narration.
- **Brand mark:** The fragmented aperture mark appears prominently in the AURA reveal, digital-twin core, navigation, and final signature—not as ornamental decoration.
